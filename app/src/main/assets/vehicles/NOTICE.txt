NavGate navigation vehicles

Sedan, pickup, SUV, hatchback and coupe: original models by Quaternius, downloaded from the individual Poly Pizza pages listed in catalog.json. Each source page identifies the model as CC0 1.0.

Changes: uniform scale, ground-centred pivot, forward axis normalization, named body paint and PBR material adjustments. Source meshes and original download hashes are recorded in catalog.json.

Navigation arrow: original NavGate geometry, dedicated under CC0 1.0.
